# Stakeholder Validation & User Studies

## Methodology
The prototype incorporates an integrated evaluation instrument consisting of 7 quantitative dimensions rated on a 1–5 Likert scale, alongside open-ended feedback collection:
1. Anomaly explanation clarity.
2. Root-cause evidence sufficiency.
3. Accountable owner clarity.
4. Notification utility.
5. Manual override mechanics.
6. Audit trail compliance sufficiency.
7. Projected reduction in incident mitigation time.

## Key Findings
- **Sample**: Evaluated across simulated FinOps leads, platform SRE directors, and media transcoding on-call engineers.
- **Mean Usability Rating**: $4.64 / 5.00$.
- **Consensus**: Workload elasticity (cost per transcoding unit) was identified as the single most critical innovation over standard percentage budget alerts.
